01 — FOUNDATION

Who we are

WOW is South Africa’s industry-owned digital operating system for the minibus taxi economy. It was built by the Eastern Cape Transport Tertiary Co-operative (ECTTC) and belongs to the people who operate it.

Crafted and owned by the public transport industry. Ten focus areas. One wallet. One owner.

~105 000Minibus taxis on the network

~15 000E-hailing vehicles in the co-op

Brand pillars

Every piece of WOW communication should prove at least one of these.

Owned from within

The platform, the wallet, the data and the revenue belong to the co-operative, not to a bank or an overseas app.

Organised as one

67 000 operators negotiating, paying and earning as a single fleet. Scale is our proof.

Built for the rank

Help happens where the industry works, on any phone, in the commuter’s own language.

Proudly South African

Township-fluent and pan-South African. We are not a foreign brand passing through.

Siyabangena! — our rallying cry

It means “we’re getting in”, with confidence and intent. The root verb -ngena, to enter, is the everyday verb of taxi life: you ngena a taxi when you board. It reads instantly in isiZulu and isiXhosa.

Siyabangena always sits with the logo in its badge, and closes our biggest messages. It is never used as a polite greeting.

03 — COLOUR

Red leads. Always.

WOW Red is the brand, and Tar and White do the hard work around it. Four focus-area accents help people find their way between products, but they never take over. Click any swatch to copy its HEX value.

Neutrals

These are warm greys mixed toward WOW Red, so they sit comfortably beside it. Don’t swap them for generic cool greys.

Focus-area accents

Each colour marks a group of focus areas on the website, in the app and on product material. Use them as labels, section grounds and illustration fills. Never recolour the logo in an accent (Tar is the exception), and never use more than one accent per layout.

What changed from 2025: the old Blue, Green, Yellow, Pink and Noir used the exact HEX values of a well-known tech company’s palette (#5865F2, #57F287, #FEE75C, #EB459E, #23272A). They have been replaced with WOW’s own colours, named after taxi-rank life. WOW Red stays the same as the logo files. Oxblood is the old Brown, renamed.

Setting headlines

✓ Do

  • Use sentence case, and end with a full stop for punch: “One wallet. One owner.”
  • Highlight one key word or phrase in WOW Red, like “Crafted and owned”.
  • Keep headlines to eight words or fewer, and break lines by meaning.
  • Use all caps only for outdoor headlines of five words or fewer.

✕ Don’t

  • Set a whole website headline in caps or mix bold and caps, as the mock-up does.
  • Highlight more than one idea in red per headline.
  • Use Archivo for body copy, or Figtree for display headlines.
  • Track headlines out or add outlines, gradients or shadows.
05 — GRAPHIC ELEMENTS

The Tab & the Route

WOW uses two brand shapes. The Tab holds our voice: logos, labels and hashtags. The Route brings energy: a bold, road-like zigzag drawn from the corridors our taxis drive every day.

#BeWOW#SiyabangenaWOW FUEL
  • The Tab is for the brand speaking: logo holders, product labels, hashtags, Siyabangena.
  • Full pills are for things you can press: buttons and filters. Keeping them separate makes interactive elements easy to spot.
  • The tight corner always sits top-right, pointing forward like the “!”.

The Route

One thick zigzag with rounded ends, bleeding off at least two edges. Use two colours from the palette: the Route in a brighter tone over a deeper ground, or Red over a light ground.

✓ Do

  • Use one Route per layout, behind a headline or photo edge.
  • Scale the stroke so it’s roughly as thick as the wordmark’s letter strokes.
  • Animate it drawing on over 600–900 ms in motion work.

✕ Don’t

  • Run it behind body text or the logo.
  • Stack several Routes or cross it over itself.
  • Add gradients, outlines or drop shadows.

Photo frames & characters

#BeWOW
#Siyabangena
  • Photos sit in 24 px rounded frames on screen, or 6 mm in print. Never use circles or hard corners.
  • The hashtag Tab docks into the bottom-left corner, flush with the frame.
  • Character concepts (the waving round “O” figures from the 2025 guide) are still in development. Use only approved master artwork, always in single-colour Red line style, and never alongside real people in the same frame.
  • The illustrations here are placeholders. Use real photography, following chapter 06.
06 — PHOTOGRAPHY

Real ranks. Real people.

Our photography shows the industry as it really is: proud, busy, sunlit and South African. People come first and vehicles second. The 2025 guide’s stock images of international teenagers don’t represent WOW and should be retired.

The owner at golden hourDriver or owner beside their Quantum, phone in hand, warm low sun. Shot from a confident low angle.
Fuel at the forecourtA WOW tag at the pump in Komani. Show the attendant and the driver together.
Paying without cashA commuter taps, scans a QR code or dials USSD on a basic phone. Show the hands and a smile, not a screen close-up.
The agent at the rankA WOW Agent in branded kit, signing up a driver or selling a SIM. Show a real conversation.
Scholars boardingLearners boarding in uniform with a palm scan at the door. Faces only with signed parental consent.
The spaza ownerA merchant behind the counter with a WOW card machine, stock on the shelves and a customer paying.

✓ Do

  • Shoot on location in the Eastern Cape: Gqeberha, Mthatha, Komani, East London.
  • Use natural daylight, rich warm tones and a real sense of movement.
  • Get signed model releases for everyone, and parental consent for all minors.
  • Leave clear space on one side for a headline and a Tab.

✕ Don’t

  • Use generic or overseas stock, or AI-generated people.
  • Show crowding, conflict, cash in hand or damaged vehicles.
  • Use heavy filters, black-and-white or duotones other than Red.
  • Show readable number plates or real commuters’ phone screens.
07 — VOICE & TONE

Arrive with intent

WOW speaks like a proud owner addressing their own people: direct, warm, confident and never corporate. We write for someone reading on a basic phone at a busy rank.

Speak as the owner

The industry built this. Say “your co-op” and “we”, never “the beneficiaries”.

✓ SayYour co-op. Your rules. Every rebate flows back to you.
✕ NotThis initiative empowers informal-sector stakeholders to access benefits.

Say it at rank level

Use short sentences and everyday words. If a driver can’t repeat it to a passenger, rewrite it.

✓ SayData that doesn’t expire. Buy what you need, when you can.
✕ NotLeverage our non-expiring MVNO data bundles for optimised connectivity.

Confident, not hype

Our proof is scale, ownership and real partners. State facts plainly and let the numbers do the shouting.

✓ Say67 000 operators. One fleet. The fuel you already buy now pays you back.
✕ NotThe most revolutionary, game-changing fintech disruption Africa has ever seen!!!

Proudly local

Use South African English, name places the way locals do, and use isiXhosa and isiZulu where it comes naturally. Always add a translation nearby for mixed audiences.

✓ SayLaunching in Komani first, then province-wide. Siyabangena!
✕ NotRolling out to select Tier-2 markets in Q3.
